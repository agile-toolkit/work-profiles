### Work Profiles — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the lightweight answer to skill and capacity mapping — the thing teams
use because the HR system is too heavy to bother with.

#### Why this app can win it

Formal skill matrices are universally disliked and rarely current. A version
that takes five minutes and feeds other tools in the suite gets kept up to date,
which is the only property that matters for this kind of data.

#### Signals

1. Profiles kept current — edits after the initial creation.
2. Capacity and skill data consumed by other apps in the suite.
3. Team-wide completion rather than one enthusiast filling it in.
4. GitHub stars.

#### Boundaries

- Never a system of record for HR.
- No personal data beyond skills, capacity, and availability.
- Not an evaluation or ranking tool.
